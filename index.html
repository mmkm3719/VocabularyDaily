Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vocabulary Daily ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve your app experience:

  • App Usage & Performance Data: Your streak counts, grade progression, and quiz statistics stored locally on your device or via cloud services to keep track of your learning journey.
  • Push Notification Tokens (FCM): Device tokens generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging to send daily study reminders and vocabulary practice alerts.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic logs and Advertising IDs collected by third-party services for crash reports and app performance monitoring.

2. Third-Party Services

Vocabulary Daily uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or show relevant advertisements. You can view their respective privacy policies below:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging

3. Advertising Policy

Our app displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. These services may use anonymized advertising identifiers to display relevant ads or limit the frequency of ads shown.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences, including students. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in using our app. We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting to this page.
