Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vocabulary Daily ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. User Accounts & Data Storage

  • No User Logins: Vocabulary Daily does not require user accounts, logins, or registration to access the app.
  • Local Data Storage: All your personal learning progress—including streak counts, quiz results, grade progress, and app preferences—is stored locally on your device via local storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our external servers.
  • Quiz Questions & Content: App content, such as daily vocabulary words and quiz questions, is retrieved dynamically over the internet. These network requests fetch static quiz material only and do not transmit your quiz scores, personal answers, or user identifiers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal technical data necessary to deliver daily notifications and support app operations:

  • Push Notification Tokens (FCM): Randomly generated device tokens provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging to schedule and deliver daily study reminders and vocabulary alerts.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Anonymized diagnostic logs, crash reports, and advertising identifiers collected by integrated third-party SDKs to monitor app performance and serve advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services

Vocabulary Daily integrates with third-party service providers that process technical device data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging

4. Advertising Policy

Our app displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. These ad networks may use anonymized advertising identifiers to display relevant ads, manage ad frequency, or comply with regional consent choices (such as GDPR and US State Privacy Laws).

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences and students. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. Because all quiz data and user settings remain stored locally on your device, no personal profiles are maintained.

If you believe a third-party SDK integrated into our app has inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us so we can assist in addressing it or guide you on clearing local app data.

6. Data Security

We value your trust. While local data resides securely on your personal device, third-party network communications utilize industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
